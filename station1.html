📚 Quick Guide: IN / ON / AT

AT — Point (at the station, at home)
ON — Surface (on the table, on the wall)
IN — 3D Space (in the box, in the room)

IN = inside something (3D space)

in the room, in the box, in the car

ON = on the surface

on the table, on the wall, on the floor

AT = specific point or location

at the station, at home, at work
